Legally establishes that invoices exceeding agreed payment terms automatically accrue late penalties, eliminating awkward negotiation when payments stall.

"Invoices unpaid after 14 calendar days from the due date shall accrue a late fee of 1.5% compounding per month (or the maximum allowed by applicable law) on the outstanding balance, plus reasonable costs and legal fees incurred in collection."

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about setting up Ledgerly for your boutique agency.

Can I issue invoices in multiple currencies like USD, EUR, and INR? +
Yes. Ledgerly supports multi-currency invoicing with real-time exchange rates and localized tax compliance presets (such as GST in India, VAT across Europe, and state sales taxes in the US). You can set distinct currency defaults per client account.
How do automated payment reminders work without annoying clients? +
Reminders are written in a calm, professional tone and are sent on pre-configured schedules (e.g. 3 days prior to due date, on the due date, and 3 days post-due). Each nudge includes a one-click payment link and a PDF download. You can pause or customize schedules per invoice at any time.
Does Ledgerly take a percentage fee on my collected payments? +
Zero percent. Ledgerly does not hold your funds or take any cut of your transactions. You connect your own payment gateway (such as Stripe or local direct bank transfers) and pay standard gateway processing fees directly to them.
Can I invite my external accountant or bookkeeper with read-only permissions? +
Yes. Studio and Agency plans include team seats with customizable roles. You can invite your external tax advisor, bookkeeper, or business partner with read-only access to export reports and inspect reconciliation ledgers without creating invoices.
